Hi, I'm Tan Zi Siang, an Interactive Multimedia Technology student passionate about bridging the gap between technology and visual storytelling.

I specialize in creating engaging digital content by combining creative design with functional technology. From photography and videography to multimedia design and web development, I enjoy transforming ideas into meaningful digital experiences.
